New campaign

Upload recipients, drop in your email copies, choose which accounts send and how the batches are spaced.

1 · Recipients

Upload a CSV (two columns: name,email — a header row is detected automatically) and/or paste recipients manually below. Both are combined, duplicates by email are removed.

2 · Email copies

Add up to 5 variations. They rotate in order across each account's sends — copy 1, 2, 3… then back to 1. Use {{name}} anywhere you want the recipient's name inserted.

3 · Sending accounts & draft counts

Pick which connected accounts take part, and how many drafts each one creates (max 100). You'll send each one manually from the account's own Drafts folder.

4 · Timing

All drafts are created as fast as the mail servers allow — no spacing needed, since nothing is actually sent until you do it yourself.
